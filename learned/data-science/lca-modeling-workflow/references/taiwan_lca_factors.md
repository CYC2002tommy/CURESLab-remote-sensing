# Taiwan LCA Emission Factors (Pre-Researched)

This reference file contains verified emission factors for the Taiwan context to save time on academic searches.

## 1. Electricity
- **Value**: `0.474 kg CO2e / kWh`
- **Context**: 113 ROC (2024) Taiwan official electricity grid emission factor.
- **Source**: Taiwan Ministry of Economic Affairs / Bureau of Energy.

## 2. Transportation (Heavy-Duty Diesel Truck, >15t)
- **Value**: `0.111 kg CO2e / ton-km`
- **Context**: Used for calculating domestic long-haul material and product transportation.
- **Source**: Taiwan EPA (Environmental Protection Administration) Carbon Footprint Calculation Platform (碳足跡產品類別規則 PCR預設運輸排放係數).

## 3. Electricity Acidification Potential (AP)
- **Value**: `~0.0015 kg SO2e / kWh` (Approximate)
- **Context**: Conversion factor based on Taiwan grid mix (coal/gas proportion) for SO2 and NOx equivalents.
